A hidden way we get in our own way is by constantly questioning and doubting ourselves.

Some quick examples:

Not picking a direction in life, or with your goals, because you doubt your ability to get any of them done

Getting stuck picking what to work on today, or right now, because it all feels equally important, and you doubt your ability to choose the “right” thing

Choosing something to work on, or choosing a goal, and doubting yourself immediately

Trying to do something, but not giving it your all, because of self-doubt

Not putting yourself out there publicly, or in front of a group, because you doubt yourself

Do any of these feel familiar?

In truth, self-doubt is hiding in every corner for many people, and it the main thing tripping them up each day.

So how can we work with self-doubt?

Start by noticing that you have it. Take on a noticing practice and try to spot it when it creeps in, when you’re stuck in self-doubt, when you’re second-guessing yourself. This will bring it more and more into your awareness.

Then start to notice what’s underneath the self-doubt. What are you feeling when you start to question yourself? Can you just be present with the feeling?
